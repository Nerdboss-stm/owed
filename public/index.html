OWED

OWED collects overdue freelance invoices, and rehearses every email before it is allowed to send one.

I am the freelancer →
Priya's desk: see what the agent wants to send, approve it.
I am the client →
Get a real email from the agent, reply, try to break it, pay.
Open my own desk →
Four overdue clients in your name, chased to your inbox. Approve, reply, rehearse again.
1 The overdue ledger, read live from Stripe.
2 Rehearse in a shadow inbox. A second model checks every draft.
3 Approve: the ledger is re-verified, then it sends. Nothing sends without a tap.

Demo runs on Priya's real test accounts. You can also open your own desk.
